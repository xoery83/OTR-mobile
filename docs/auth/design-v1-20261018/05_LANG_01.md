# OTR-LANG-01 — Multilingual Capture & Identity Resolution V1.0
2026-10-18 | Horizontal product contract and audit request, not full implementation authorization.

## Principle
App UI language, source document language, name script, destination and device locale are independent. Mixed-language documents are normal. Preserve original Unicode source and evidence, while mapping interpretation into existing Canonical fields. No alternate Admission engine or reduced evidence threshold.

## Three identity layers
1. **Original:** retain raw spelling, script, order, punctuation and source evidence (e.g. 李力, LI/LI). 2. **Derived normalization:** case, whitespace, width, separators, airline surname/given format, transliteration candidates; not automatically authoritative AKA. 3. **Confirmed AKA:** manually confirmed or learned on authorized Account–Participant Connect, with source, dedup, deletion suppression and correction. Cross-Trip confirmed AKA can automatically resolve ordinary unambiguous Capture name matching; ambiguous or permission-affecting claims require Review/authorization.

## Pipeline checks
- Source/OCR: original bytes/text, mixed scripts, evidence span/location and Unicode integrity retained.
- Interpretation: typed semantic fields with raw value and source span, language-aware extraction, uncertainty preserved; no fabricated date/year/airport/timezone.
- Location: multilingual aliases to trusted place identities; `东京/東京/Tokyo` may share geography but not by translation alone. TIME-01 timezone/floating rules apply.
- Dates: region-specific formats interpreted from source, not device locale; Date-only, Floating, exact local time remain distinct.
- Currency: preserve symbol and original numeric formatting; ¥ may be CNY or JPY, no blind inference; Ledger authority retained.
- Name matching: Unicode forms and confirmed AKA, no automatically learned generated translations/transliterations. Multiple matching Persons require focused Review.
- Review: explanation in UI language, original source evidence visible; no forced language picker or duplicate confirmation for safe known AKA.
- Admission: CP12/CP13/W0 source proof, lineage, idempotency, permissions and Canonical identity unchanged.

## Delivery phases
**LANG-01A now:** read-only compatibility audit; repair irreversible source/Unicode/evidence loss only after Coordinator approval. **LANG-01B after Capture E2E:** multilingual Interpretation, names/places, targeted Review. **LANG-01C Beta QA:** mixed-language fixtures, ambiguous dates/currency, offline and Native validation. No global multilingual engine/schema by default.

## Acceptance fixtures
Chinese name in Japanese hotel booking; LI/LI and 李力; two same-name Participants; 东京/東京/Tokyo; ¥ ambiguous currency; full-width characters; mixed Japanese/English; day/month ambiguity; date-only and floating timezone; confirmed AKA auto-match without granting membership. Preserve source-to-admission evidence trace.

## Open technical decisions
Existing Unicode storage, OCR/model provider language coverage, normalization implementation, AKA provenance schema, translation policy, cost, privacy and locale UI coverage must be audited, not assumed implemented.
