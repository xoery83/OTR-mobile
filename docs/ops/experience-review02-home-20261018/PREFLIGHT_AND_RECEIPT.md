## Important date distinction

`TripScreen.widgetsForHome` currently passes unknown-zone `planningDate` from DEV perspective, saved Day view, saved resume view, then Core Trip start. Thus an unknown-zone Home opened on Oct 18 may intentionally select Oct 16 as its **plan date**, and legitimately find a stay that covered Oct 16. The night filter is not by itself evidence that an Oct 17 checkout remains active on Oct 18. No device timezone/date substitution is introduced. Coordinator must preserve readable selected date and ensure the surrounding heading never claims clock-today for this fallback.
